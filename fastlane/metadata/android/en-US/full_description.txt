Discover related reporting from newsrooms, specialist publications and independent blogs.

Paste an article link. Freilesen reads available page metadata and searches news indexes, public RSS feeds and blog platforms for other articles about the same subject. German-language links can also lead to English sources, and English links can lead to German sources.

• Compare related articles with a topic-overlap score.
• Filter results by German, English or both languages.
• Save links into your own topic folders.
• Open the original article at its publisher.

Freilesen does not bypass paywalls and does not reproduce full articles. A result is an independent perspective, not necessarily a word-for-word or complete substitute. The score compares available titles and metadata, not the full article. Access can change; unclear access is marked as uncertain and should be checked at the publisher.

Searching needs an internet connection and the Freilesen search service. Article metadata is fetched by that service; topic search terms are sent to Google News RSS and GDELT. The app does not include advertising or analytics SDKs.

Freilesen is free to use. An optional coffee donation link is available in the app.
